Use the provided image as the identity reference. Preserve the same face, hairstyle, age, body proportions and outfit. Action: she looks at the camera and naturally raises the product. Camera: medium close-up, smartphone UGC camera, eye level. Movement: subtle handheld motion, natural blinking and micro-movements. Environment: same room, same lighting. Restrictions: no face change, no morphing, no exaggerated gestures.

Control de consistencia
07 / 12

Antes de editar: revisar identidad

Debe mantenerse

Rostro, edad aparente, cabello, proporciones, ropa base y tono visual.

Se corta si falla

Manos raras, cara distinta, mirada perdida, producto deformado o gesto exagerado.

Si el clip no sostiene la identidad, no se arregla en edición. Se regenera.
